Good to know

How do I order?

Add products to your basket, open the basket, fill in your name, phone and delivery address, and send your order. We will call you to confirm it.

How do I pay?

Payment is cash on delivery only. There is no online or card payment.

When will my order arrive?

When we call to confirm your order, we agree the delivery details and timing with you.

Are the products suitable for sensitive skin and children?

Kernel began with a formula for a newborn’s delicate skin. Each product page describes what it is for, and we are happy to advise you by message or at a 1-to-1 appointment.

Can you customize a product for me?

Yes. Tell us about your skin needs through the contact page and Rita will get back to you.

How fresh are the products?

Everything is handcrafted in small batches, so your order is made fresh.
